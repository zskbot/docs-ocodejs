# Quickstart: From sign-up to your first code review

> Create your Qodo account, link your Git account, and get automated code reviews on your pull requests.

Create your Qodo account and start receiving automated code reviews on your pull requests.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Are you an organization admin?** Set up your organization first. See [Set up your Qodo organization as an admin](https://docs.qodo.ai/set-up-your-qodo-organization.md).

## Step 1: Create your account

Go to [app.qodo.ai/signin](https://app.qodo.ai/signin) and choose how you'd like to sign in.

> **Continue with Google**
>
> Select your Google account and you'll be redirected back to Qodo automatically.

> **Continue with GitHub**
>
> Enter your GitHub credentials, click **Authorize**, and you're in.

> **Continue with Email**
>
> Enter your email, check your inbox for a verification link, and click to activate.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Invited by your team?** Open the email with subject **"Join your team – enjoy Qodo"**, click **Accept the invite**, then sign in with the same email address that received the invitation.

## Step 2: Link your Git account

Linking your Git account lets Qodo identify your pull requests and commits.

**Step 1**

Click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar.

**Step 2**

Select **Link your Git user account**.

See [Account management](https://docs.qodo.ai/account-management/manage-your-qodo-account.md#additional-settings).

## Step 3: Open a pull request

Qodo automatically reviews every pull request in your organization's connected repositories. Open a pull request and Qodo will:

> **Apply your rules**
>
> Your team's standards enforced on every change, automatically.

> **Surface findings**
>
> Bugs, risks, and standard violations, explained and prioritized.

> **Analyze your changes**
>
> Multi-agent review across your entire codebase, not just the diff.
